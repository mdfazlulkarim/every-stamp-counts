      Why it matters

      The rules are set between states. The paperwork lands on people.

      Whether you need a visa is decided by the authorities of the place you visit, often through agreements between states. It says nothing about you.

      Yet for many travellers, one trip means forms, fees, bank statements, invitation letters, embassy queues and weeks of waiting, with no promise of a yes. This site is a small reminder that some stamps take far more effort than others.

      About the data and the map
      How the score works
      Each country you have visited earns points for the door your passport met there.
      Visa rules
      From visa-matrix, which reads the Wikipedia pages on visa requirements and cross-checks each one with Passport Index.
      Official rank
      Henley Passport Index by Henley & Partners, based on IATA data.
      Map
      Country shapes from Natural Earth, drawn to follow the conventions of United Nations maps. Small countries are shown as dots.

      Limits
      The rules shown are current rules, not the rules on the day you travelled. Residence permits and visas you hold for other countries are not counted. If you hold more than one passport, choose the one you travel on most.
      Privacy
      Your choices stay in your browser. Nothing is sent or stored anywhere else.
